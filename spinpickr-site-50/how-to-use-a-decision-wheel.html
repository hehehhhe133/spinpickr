How to Use a Decision Wheel

A decision wheel is a simple tool: you list your options, spin, and accept the result. It works because it removes the effort of choosing between choices that are all acceptable.

When a wheel works well

  • Choices where every option is fine, such as dinner, a movie or a weekend plan.
  • Fairness tasks, such as picking a student, a team or who pays.
  • Breaking a tie when a group cannot agree.

When to be careful

Avoid using a wheel for serious decisions about health, money or safety. Use it for small choices and keep those important choices for careful thought.

Tips for better wheels

  • Only add options you would truly accept.
  • Type an option twice to make it more likely.
  • Remove a result after it is picked if you want no repeats.
  • Agree in advance that the result is final.
