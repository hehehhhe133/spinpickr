About SpinPickr

SpinPickr is a free decision wheel and random picker. It was built to take the effort out of small choices, like what to eat, who goes first, or which team a student joins. You type your options, press spin and accept the result.

What you can do here

The home page gives you a blank wheel that you can fill with anything. Beyond that, we maintain ready-made wheels in four groups: food, classroom, fun and everyday decisions and seasonal. Each wheel has a starter list that you can edit, along with ideas, tips and answers to common questions about using it.

How it works

Each spin uses your browser's random number generator, and every option has the same chance. If you want an option to come up more often, type it on more than one line. Wheels run entirely in your browser: we do not ask you to sign up, and the options you type are not stored or sent to us.

Who it is for

Teachers use it to pick students, make groups and plan activities. Families use it for dinner, chores and weekend plans. Friends use it to settle small arguments and run games. It is meant for everyday, low-stakes choices; for important decisions about health, money or safety, we recommend careful thought and advice from people you trust.

Keeping it free

The site is supported by advertising, which keeps every wheel free to use. You can read how we handle data and cookies on the privacy page.
