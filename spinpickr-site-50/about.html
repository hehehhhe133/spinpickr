About

SpinPickr is a free decision wheel and random picker. It was built to take the effort out of small choices, like what to eat, who goes first, or which team a student joins.

Our wheels run entirely in your browser. We do not ask you to sign up, and we do not store the options you type. We add new ready-made wheels regularly, and we welcome suggestions through the contact page.

The site is supported by advertising, which keeps every wheel free.
