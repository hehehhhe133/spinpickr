Food Wheels

Stop asking what to eat and let a wheel pick the meal.

Food decisions are the most frequent small decisions of the day, and they are surprisingly tiring. What should we have for dinner? Where shall we order from? Which dessert? The wheels on this page turn each of those questions into a quick spin, so you spend less time debating and more time eating.

Each wheel comes with a starter list of popular choices, including Indian and international favourites, and every list is fully editable. Replace the defaults with what is in your fridge, a restaurant's menu or your family's favourite dishes.

Every wheel here is free and fully editable. Pick one, replace the options with your own and spin. Nothing is stored, so your lists stay private.

Tips for using these wheels

  • Fill the wheel with options you would genuinely be happy to eat, so any result is a good result.
  • Type an option on more than one line to improve its chances, for example to nudge the week towards healthier meals.
  • Spin once per day and write the results down to build a quick weekly meal plan.
