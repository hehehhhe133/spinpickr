What to Eat for Dinner Wheel

Spin to decide tonight's dinner. Edit the meals and spin.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Deciding what to cook after a long day is surprisingly tiring. This dinner wheel removes the debate: it starts with eight easy, common meals, and you can replace them with whatever is in your fridge or on your weekly plan.

Try filling the wheel with only meals you already have ingredients for. That way the result is never a problem, only a plan.

Ways to use it

  • Plan the week by spinning once per day and noting the results.
  • Add a 'kid's choice' slot for families.
  • Include a takeaway option for lazy nights; couples often do this.
  • Use it with a partner or flatmate so neither of you has to make the call.

Tips

  • Keep a shopping list for the meals on the wheel so ingredients are always at hand.
  • Remove meals you have eaten recently to keep the week varied.
  • Add the cooking time to each option, such as 'Pasta (20 min)', for busy evenings.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result really random?

Yes. Each spin uses your browser's random number generator and every option has an equal chance.

Can I use my own meals?

Yes. Type one meal per line in the box under the wheel, up to 15 options.

Does it remember my list?

No. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets the wheel. Copy your list somewhere if you want to reuse it.
