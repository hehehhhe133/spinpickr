Snack Picker Wheel

Hungry between meals? Let the wheel choose.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Snacking decisions are small but constant, and they tend to default to whatever is nearest. The wheel mixes light and indulgent choices so you are not always reaching for the same thing.

You can shape the odds yourself. Type an option on several lines and it gets that many slices, which is handy if you would like the healthier choices to come up more often.

Ways to use it

  • Choose an afternoon snack at work or at home.
  • Pick a movie-night snack with friends.
  • Let children spin for a healthy snack after school.
  • Use it when packing a snack box for a trip.

Making the most of it

Snack choices tend to be automatic, which is why a pause with the wheel can help. Spin before you open the cupboard, and commit to what lands for that moment. If you are trying to snack more mindfully, put the foods you want to eat more often on several lines, and keep the treats to one or two slices. For children, build a snack wheel together and let them choose the options, which gives them a sense of control while keeping the choices sensible. At the office, a shared snack wheel is a fun way to decide what goes in the cupboard for the week.

Tips

  • Put fruit, nuts and yoghurt on more slices to nudge yourself towards healthier snacks.
  • Keep the list to snacks you actually have.
  • Add portion hints such as 'Handful of nuts' to keep it balanced.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can I weight the odds?

Yes. Type an option twice to give it two slices.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, the wheel runs in your browser, so it keeps working without a new connection.

Is it a diet tool?

No. It is a fun way to decide, not nutrition advice.
