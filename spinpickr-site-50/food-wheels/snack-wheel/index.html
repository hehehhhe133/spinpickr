Snack Picker Wheel

Hungry between meals? Let the wheel choose.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Snacking decisions are small but constant. The wheel mixes light and indulgent choices so you are not always picking the same thing.

Tip

Keep healthier snacks in more slices of the wheel if you want the odds in their favour, since you can repeat an item on several lines.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Can I weight the odds?

Yes, type an option twice to give it two slices.

Does it work offline?

Once loaded, yes, as the wheel runs in your browser.
