What to Cook Today Wheel (Aaj Kya Banayein)

Spin when you cannot decide what to cook.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

'Aaj kya banayein?' is one of the most repeated questions in many homes, and it is rarely about hunger. It is about choosing, over and over, from the same few dishes. The What to Cook Today Wheel takes that decision off your plate and replaces it with a single spin.

The starter list holds simple Indian meals that most kitchens can manage on a weekday: dal chawal, rajma chawal, pulao, khichdi, pav bhaji and more. Replace it with your own family favourites and whatever vegetables or dals are in your kitchen.

Ways to use it

  • Spin in the afternoon so you can soak dal or rajma in time.
  • Plan the week by spinning once for each day and noting the results.
  • Add dishes of your region or festival specials to keep the list personal.
  • Let the children or family members spin, so dinner becomes a shared decision.

Making the most of it

Meal planning does not have to be rigid to save time and money. A quick routine is to spin on Sunday for the week, write the results on a note on the fridge and buy vegetables for those dishes in one trip. Families with different tastes can keep two lists, one for everyday meals and one for treats, and let the wheel choose from the right list for the day. If you cook for elders or children, add notes such as 'less spicy' or 'with curd' next to the options. And on days when no result feels right, treat the wheel as a starting point, choose the nearest dish on your list and move on.

Tips

  • Fill the wheel only with dishes you have ingredients for, so the result is never a problem.
  • Mix quick meals, like khichdi and poha, with longer ones, like rajma, and keep the longer ones for weekends.
  • Add a 'leftovers' slice for the days when the fridge needs clearing.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Does it only have Indian dishes?

The default list is Indian, but you can replace it with any cuisine you like.

Can I plan a full week?

Yes. Spin once per day and write the results down.

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes. Add up to 15 dishes, one per line.
