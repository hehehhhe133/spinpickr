Cuisine Picker Wheel

Spin to choose what kind of food to eat.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Choosing a restaurant is often easier in two steps: first the type of food, then the place. The Cuisine Picker Wheel handles the first step and cuts the options down to a size you can manage. Once you know the cuisine, searching for a nearby restaurant takes far less time and far less arguing.

The starter list includes eight popular cuisines. Replace them with the ones you like or the ones available near you, for example North Indian, South Indian, biryani, street food or Korean.

Ways to use it

  • Pick the cuisine for a dinner out, then search for places nearby.
  • Choose a takeaway cuisine for a night in.
  • Spin for a cooking challenge at home and look for a recipe from that cuisine.
  • Settle group decisions at work lunches and family outings.

Making the most of it

Trying a cuisine you rarely choose can be a small adventure. If the wheel lands on one you have never eaten, look up two or three popular dishes beforehand so you can order with confidence. Groups that eat out regularly can keep a record of the cuisines that came up and cross them off, so the wheel nudges everyone towards variety. For vegetarians, vegans or people with allergies, make sure the cuisines on the wheel all have good options; Indian, Mediterranean and Thai usually do. And if the team cannot agree on a restaurant, spin a second wheel with the names of two or three shortlisted places.

Tips

  • Remove any cuisine that someone cannot eat for dietary or religious reasons before spinning.
  • Add regional cuisines from your own city to discover new local places.
  • Agree that the result is final, or give each person one veto before the spin.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Does it pick a restaurant?

No. It picks the cuisine, and you then choose a place that serves it.

Can I add local cuisines?

Yes. Add regional or local cuisines to the list.

Does it work for groups?

Yes. Agree beforehand that the result is final.
