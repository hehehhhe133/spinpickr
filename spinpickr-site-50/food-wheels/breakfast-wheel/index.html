Breakfast Ideas Wheel

Spin to choose what to have for breakfast.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Breakfast is the meal most people repeat without thinking. This wheel mixes quick Indian and Western options so the morning has a little variety.

Tip

Put your five fastest options on the wheel for weekdays and your slower favourites for weekends.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Can I use it for meal prep?

Yes, spin once for each day of the week and note the results.

Is anything saved?

No, refresh the page to reset.
