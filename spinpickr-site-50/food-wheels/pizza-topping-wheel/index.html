Pizza Topping Wheel

Let the wheel build your pizza, one topping at a time.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Pizza night gets more fun when nobody gets to choose. Spin the wheel two or three times to build a random pizza, or let each person in the family spin once for their own slice.

Ideas and tips

Make it a game: any topping you land on has to go on the pizza, no vetoes. You can also swap in sauces, crusts or dips for a different challenge.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again for another result, or change the list first.

FAQ

Can I add sauces or crusts?

Yes. The wheel accepts any text, so use it for sauces, crust types or sides.

Is it free?

Yes, free with no signup.
