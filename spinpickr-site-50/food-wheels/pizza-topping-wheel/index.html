Pizza Topping Wheel

Let the wheel build your pizza, one topping at a time.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Pizza night gets more fun when nobody gets to choose. Spin the wheel two or three times to build a random pizza, or let each person in the family spin once for their own slice.

The default list mixes classic and Indian-style toppings, such as pepperoni, mushrooms and paneer, and you can replace it with whatever your pizzeria offers.

Ways to use it

  • Make it a rule that any topping you land on has to go on the pizza, no vetoes.
  • Let each guest spin for a topping at a party.
  • Swap in sauces, crusts or dips for a different challenge.
  • Use it to decide toppings for a homemade pizza.

Making the most of it

Random toppings make for surprising pizzas, and a few simple rules keep them tasty. Limit yourself to three toppings, balance one vegetable with one protein and one cheese, and avoid toppings that are very wet, which can make the base soggy. If you are cooking at home, use the wheel to choose a sauce as well, such as tomato, pesto or white sauce. For a party, give every guest a small base and let them spin for their own toppings, then bake them all together and vote on the most creative one. Remember to check for allergies first.

Tips

  • Cap it at three toppings so the pizza still tastes good.
  • Remove any ingredients that someone is allergic to before the first spin.
  • Add 'Extra cheese' on more than one line to improve its odds.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add sauces or crusts?

Yes. The wheel accepts any text, so use it for sauces, crust types or sides.

Is it free?

Yes, free with no signup.

Can I use it to decide where to order from?

Yes. Replace the toppings with restaurant names.
