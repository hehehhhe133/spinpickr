Ice Cream Flavor Wheel

Spin to pick your next scoop.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Standing in front of a freezer full of flavours, most people freeze or pick the same one they always do. The wheel makes the call for you and gently nudges you towards something new.

The starter list combines classics with Indian favourites such as mango and butterscotch. Replace them with the menu at your local shop or the tubs in your freezer.

Ways to use it

  • Choose a flavour at an ice cream parlour when you cannot decide.
  • Let each child spin for their own scoop on a family outing.
  • Pick the flavour for a homemade ice cream batch.
  • Add toppings or cone types to a second wheel for a full sundae challenge.

Making the most of it

Trying a new flavour is one of the cheapest adventures available. The wheel turns it into a small game: you promise to eat whatever lands, and in return you get to taste something you might never have picked. Many people find that they enjoy at least half of the surprise flavours. For ice cream tastings with friends, give each person a different scoop and compare notes, then add the winners to your regular list. If you make ice cream at home, use the wheel to choose a base flavour and a mix-in, such as mango with pistachio or coffee with chocolate chips.

Tips

  • Allow one re-spin if you really dislike the result.
  • Add the shop's seasonal flavours for extra variety.
  • Use it with friends to pick a shared tub.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. Replace the list with your shop's menu.

Yes, with no signup.
