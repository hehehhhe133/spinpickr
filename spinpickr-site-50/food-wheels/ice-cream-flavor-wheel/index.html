Ice Cream Flavor Wheel

Spin to pick your next scoop.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Faced with a wall of flavours, most people freeze or pick the same one. The wheel makes the choice for you and nudges you to try something new.

Tip

Try the rule that whatever lands is your flavour, with one re-spin allowed if you truly hate it.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Can I use local flavours?

Yes, replace the list with the shop menu.

Is it free?

Yes, with no signup.
