Dessert Picker Wheel

Choose a dessert without the usual debate.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Dessert should be the easy part of a meal, yet groups often spend longer picking it than eating it. The wheel ends the debate with a clear result that everyone can see, which is why it works so well at family dinners and get-togethers.

The default list mixes global favourites with Indian sweets such as kheer, halwa and gulab jamun. Edit it to reflect your own menu, a party spread or the ingredients already in your cupboard.

Ways to use it

  • Pick the dessert for a family dinner or celebration.
  • At a party, spin for the dessert each guest brings.
  • Use it when baking to choose the next recipe on your wish list.
  • Add the dessert menu of a restaurant or cafe for a night out.

Making the most of it

A dessert wheel is useful at a dinner party, where the host does not want to choose between everyone's favourites. List the three or four things you can serve, add a surprise slice for the guests to enjoy, and let the wheel decide. If you are baking for a large gathering, spin for the flavour and the format separately, such as chocolate with cupcakes. For smaller households, build a weekly dessert plan so treats stay occasional and special. And if one of the options is particularly time-consuming, save it for weekends, so the wheel gives you only dishes you can really make.

Tips

  • Add a 'fruit' slice if you want a lighter option in the mix.
  • Agree before the spin that the result is final.
  • If anyone has allergies, remove unsuitable items before spinning.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add recipes?

Yes. Any text works, one item per line.

How many options are allowed?

Up to 15.

Can I use it for baking challenges?

Yes. Spin for a flavour, then spin again for the type of dessert.
