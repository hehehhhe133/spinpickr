Dessert Picker Wheel

Choose a dessert without the usual debate.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Dessert is meant to be fun, so let the wheel settle disagreements. It works for family dinners, parties and solo cravings alike.

Tip

At a party, spin for the dessert each guest brings, or use it when baking to pick the next recipe.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Can I add recipes?

Yes, any text works, one item per line.

How many options are allowed?

Up to 15.
