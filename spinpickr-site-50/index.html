How the decision wheel works

SpinPickr is a free decision wheel and random picker. Type your options into the box under the wheel, one per line, then press the centre button to spin. The wheel slows down on a single slice and shows you the result. It takes a few seconds, and it works for almost any choice: what to eat, who goes first, which team a student joins, or where to go on Saturday.

Every option has the same chance of winning, and the choice comes from your browser's random number generator, not from a person. Nothing you type is saved or sent to a server, so you can use real names, plans and ideas without worrying about where they end up.

Why use a wheel to decide?

Small decisions are often harder than they should be. When two options feel equally good, thinking longer rarely helps, and arguments about who chooses can take more time than the decision itself. A wheel moves the choice outside the group. Everyone can watch the spin, nobody has to give in, and the result feels fair because it is.

A wheel also shows you what you really want. If the result makes you glad, go with it. If you feel disappointed, that reaction is useful information, and you can pick the option you were hoping for instead. It is a simple way to break a tie without any pressure.

Tips for better results

  • Fill the wheel only with options you would be happy with, so any result is a good result.
  • Type an option on more than one line to give it a bigger chance, for example to make a healthier meal come up more often.
  • Agree before you spin that the result is final. It prevents arguments afterwards.
  • Remove an option after it wins if you want everyone to get a turn, as with picking students or assigning chores.
  • Keep important decisions about health, money or safety away from the wheel, and use it for everyday choices.

Frequently asked questions

Is SpinPickr free?

Yes. Every wheel is free to use, with no signup, no account and no limit on the number of spins.

Is the result really random?

Yes. Each spin uses your browser's random number generator, and every option on the wheel has the same chance of winning. Spinning harder or longer does not change the odds.

Are my options saved or sent anywhere?

No. What you type stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and the list resets when you refresh or close the page.

How many options can I add?

You can add from 2 to 15 options, one per line. For a longer list, split it across two wheels, for example by group or category.

How do I make one option more likely to win?

Type it on more than one line. An option entered twice gets two slices, so it has double the chance of coming up.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The wheel adapts to any screen size and works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute or unmute the spin sound. The setting is remembered in your browser.

Can I use it in a classroom or at work?

Yes. Teachers use it to choose students and make groups, and teams use it for icebreakers and quick decisions. Open it on a projector or shared screen so everyone can watch the spin.
