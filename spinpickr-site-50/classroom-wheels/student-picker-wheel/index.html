Random Student Picker Wheel

Pick a student fairly for any classroom task.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Calling on students at random keeps the whole class alert, because nobody knows who will be next. It also makes the choice feel fair, so students are less likely to feel picked on or ignored.

This version is set up for quick selection of a volunteer, whether you need a reader, a helper or someone to answer a question. Add the names, spin and move on in seconds.

Ways to use it

  • Pick a student to answer a quick check-for-understanding question.
  • Choose a helper to hand out books, collect papers or run an errand.
  • Select who reads the next paragraph during shared reading.
  • Use it at the end of the lesson to choose someone to summarise what was learned.

Tips

  • Remove each name after it is picked so every student gets a turn.
  • Let students know they may say 'pass' once, if that suits your classroom culture.
  • Use it for a mix of easy and harder tasks so being picked never feels like a punishment.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is it private?

Yes. Nothing leaves your browser, and names disappear when you refresh the page.

How many students can I add?

Up to 15 per wheel. Use two wheels for a bigger class.

How is this different from the name picker?

They work the same way. This page is aimed at quickly choosing a volunteer for tasks, while the name picker suits fair participation across a whole class list.
