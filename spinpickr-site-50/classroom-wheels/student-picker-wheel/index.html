Random Student Picker Wheel

Pick a student fairly for any classroom task.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Calling on students at random keeps everyone alert, and it makes choices feel fair. Unlike the name picker, this version works well for quickly choosing a volunteer for a task.

Tip

Remove a name after it is picked to give everyone a turn.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Is it private?

Yes, nothing leaves your browser.

How many students?

Up to 15 per wheel.
