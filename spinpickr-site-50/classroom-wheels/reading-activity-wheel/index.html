Reading Activity Wheel

Choose a reading task to try.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Variety keeps reading time engaging. This wheel selects a task so students try different ways to interact with a text.

Tip

Spin at the start of a session and stick to the result for 10 to 15 minutes.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ
