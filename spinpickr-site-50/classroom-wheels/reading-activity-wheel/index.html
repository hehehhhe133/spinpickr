Reading Activity Wheel

Choose a reading task to try.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Reading lessons can become repetitive when students do the same task every session. This wheel chooses a different way to engage with a text each time, such as reading aloud, summarising a page or predicting what happens next.

The default tasks suit primary and middle school, and they work with almost any book or passage. Spinning at the start of a session also saves you from deciding on the spot.

Ways to use it

  • Spin at the beginning of reading time and use the result for ten to fifteen minutes.
  • Let students spin individually in a reading corner to choose their own task.
  • Add comprehension prompts such as 'Find the main character's problem'.
  • Use it in read-aloud sessions to vary how the class interacts with the story.

Tips

  • Keep the instructions short and visible so students can start quickly.
  • Match the tasks to the text; for example, 'Act it out' works well for dialogue-heavy stories.
  • Add a quiet option for days when the class needs calm.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

What age group is it for?

Mainly primary and middle school, though the tasks can be adapted for older students.

Can I add tasks?

Yes. Replace or add tasks to suit your lesson, up to 15.

Does it work for ESL learners?

Yes. Short task names are easy to read and can be translated into the students' first language.
