Icebreaker Questions Wheel

Fun questions for any group.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Icebreakers help people relax in meetings, classes and team events. A wheel makes them playful, not forced.

Tip

Keep questions light and let people skip if they want.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.
