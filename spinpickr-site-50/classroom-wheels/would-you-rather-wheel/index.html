Would You Rather Wheel

Spin for a would-you-rather question.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

A good Would You Rather question gets everyone talking. The wheel keeps the game moving and the questions classroom-safe.

Tip

Ask students to justify their choice in one sentence.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Is it classroom appropriate?

Yes, the defaults are mild and kid-friendly.

Can I add questions?

Yes.
