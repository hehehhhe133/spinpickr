Random Name Picker for Classroom

Paste your student names and pick one fairly.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

A random name picker keeps participation fair and keeps students paying attention, because anyone could be next. Paste your class list into the box, one name per line, and spin to choose who answers, presents or leads.

Everything runs in your browser. Student names are never uploaded or stored, and they disappear as soon as you refresh or close the page, which makes it a simple option for privacy-conscious schools.

Ways to use it

  • Cold-call students for answers without favouring the ones who always raise their hands.
  • Pick a line leader, a helper of the day or the next person to read aloud.
  • Choose who presents first in a group project or an oral practice session.
  • Use it outside school too, for raffles, giveaways and deciding who goes first in a game.

Tips

  • Delete a name after it is chosen so everyone gets a turn before names repeat.
  • For classes bigger than 15, split the list into two wheels, for example by row or by table.
  • Tell students the picker is random, so it never feels like you are targeting anyone.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Are student names stored?

No. Names stay in your browser and vanish when you close or refresh the page.

How many names fit on the wheel?

Up to 15 at a time. For a larger class, make two wheels.

Is the choice truly random?

Yes. Each name has the same chance on every spin.
