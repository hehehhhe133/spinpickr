Random Name Picker for Classroom

Paste your student names and pick one fairly.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

A random name picker keeps participation fair and keeps students paying attention, because anyone could be next. Paste your class list into the box (one name per line) and spin to choose who answers, presents or leads.

Ideas and tips

Teachers often use it for cold-calling, choosing a line leader, or picking who reads next. To avoid repeats, delete a name from the list after it is chosen. Everything runs in your browser, so student names are never uploaded or stored.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again for another result, or change the list first.

FAQ

Are student names stored?

No. Names stay in your browser and disappear when you close or refresh the page.

How many names fit?

Up to 15 at a time. For a bigger class, split the list into two wheels.
