Writing Prompt Wheel

Spin for a story starter.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Blank pages stall many writers. A random prompt gives students a place to begin without the pressure of choosing.

Tip

Spin twice and combine the prompts for a stranger, more creative story.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ
