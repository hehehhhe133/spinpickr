Classroom Wheels

Name pickers, group makers and brain breaks for teachers.

Teachers make dozens of small, fair decisions every day: who answers, who presents, which group a student joins, when to take a break. A visible random wheel makes those choices quick and keeps students engaged, because anyone could be next.

The wheels here cover participation, grouping, seating, rewards, brain breaks and subject activities for reading, writing and maths. Everything runs in the browser, student names are never uploaded or stored, and there is no signup, so you can use them on a classroom screen straight away.

Every wheel here is free and fully editable. Pick one, replace the options with your own and spin. Nothing is stored, so your lists stay private.

Tips for using these wheels

  • Put the wheel on a projector or smartboard so the whole class can see that the result is random.
  • Remove names or options after they are chosen to make sure everyone gets a turn.
  • Let students operate the wheel sometimes, which builds engagement and trust in the process.
