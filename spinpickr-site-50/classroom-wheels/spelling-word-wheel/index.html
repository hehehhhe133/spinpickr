Spelling Word Wheel

Spin for a word to spell.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Spelling practice can feel dull when it is always a list read from top to bottom. A wheel adds a little suspense, because nobody knows which word is next. Students pay closer attention, and the whole class stays involved, not just the child whose turn it is.

The starter list contains words that many learners find tricky, such as 'necessary', 'separate' and 'rhythm'. Replace it with your own weekly list, a topic vocabulary list or words from a book your class is reading.

Ways to use it

  • Spin for a word and ask a student to spell it aloud or write it on the board.
  • Ask the class to use the word in a sentence after it is spelled.
  • Play in teams, scoring a point for each correct spelling.
  • Use it as a revision game before a spelling test.

Making the most of it

Spelling improves when students meet words in different ways. After the wheel picks a word, you can ask students to spell it, say it backwards, break it into syllables, find a rhyme or write a short sentence. For English language learners, add a picture or a meaning beside each word, and let students check each other in pairs before the whole class answers. Keep the pace brisk: about five to ten minutes at the start or end of a lesson is enough, and short, regular practice works better than one long session. Celebrate effort as well as accuracy, so students stay willing to try difficult words.

Tips

  • Remove words as they are used so every word on the list is practised.
  • Keep the list to 15 words or fewer; use two wheels for longer lists.
  • Offer a 'help' option, such as a letter hint, for students who are still learning.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add my own words?

Yes. Type your word list one per line, up to 15 on the wheel.

Is it suitable for all ages?

Yes. Change the words to match the grade level and subject.

Can I use it for other subjects?

Yes. Use it for maths vocabulary, science terms or foreign-language words.
