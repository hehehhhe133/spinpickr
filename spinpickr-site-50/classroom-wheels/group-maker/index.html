Group Picker Wheel

Assign students to teams or groups with a spin.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Group work lives or dies on how the groups are made. When students pick their own, the same friendships dominate; when the teacher picks, there are complaints about favouritism. A visible random draw sits neatly between the two, because everyone can see the result was not chosen by anyone.

Enter team names, colours or table numbers, then spin once for each student. When a group is full, delete it from the list and carry on until everyone has a place. The whole class can be sorted in a couple of minutes.

Ways to use it

  • Assign students to science lab stations, reading circles or project teams.
  • Choose the order of presentations, or which group starts the activity.
  • Divide a class into teams for quizzes and sports, with up to 15 groups at once.
  • Use it with adults too, for workshop breakouts, training sessions or team-building days.

Tips

  • Decide the group size in advance and remove a group name as soon as it fills.
  • Read each result out loud so the process feels open and quick.
  • If you need balanced skill levels, make the groups by ability first and use the wheel only to decide which group each pair joins.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make more than four groups?

Yes. Add up to 15 group names, one per line.

Can I use it for sports teams?

Yes. It works for any random assignment, from football teams to quiz teams.

What if a student is unhappy with the result?

Agree beforehand that the result stands. Knowing the process is random usually settles complaints quickly.
