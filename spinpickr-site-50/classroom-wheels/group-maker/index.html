Group Picker Wheel

Assign students to teams or groups with a spin.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Letting a wheel pick the group removes the "why am I not with my friends?" complaints, because everyone can see the choice is random. Add team names or colours and spin once per student.

Ideas and tips

Spin for each student and note the team that comes up. If a team is full, remove it from the list and keep going. This also works for choosing the order of presentations or lab stations.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again for another result, or change the list first.

FAQ

Can I make more than four groups?

Yes, add up to 15 group names.

Can I use it for sports teams?

Yes, it works for any random assignment.
