Show and Tell Topic Wheel

Give show and tell a theme.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Themes make show and tell easier for shy students and more varied for the class. Spin for the topic at the start of each week.

Tip

Send the theme home a day before so students have time to prepare.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Yes, mainly primary school.

Yes.
