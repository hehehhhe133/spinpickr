Show and Tell Topic Wheel

Give show and tell a theme.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Open-ended show and tell can be intimidating for shy students, and it often produces the same kinds of items week after week. A topic wheel gives every child a clear, friendly prompt and creates variety across the class.

Themes like 'something blue', 'a hobby' or 'a favourite book' are easy to interpret and rarely require buying anything. Spinning once a week gives children and families time to prepare.

Ways to use it

  • Spin on Monday and send the theme home so families can help students prepare.
  • Use it for circle time with younger children who enjoy the surprise.
  • Create a class-wide theme week, with everyone sharing something connected to the same topic.
  • Add themes linked to your current unit, such as 'Something from nature' during a science topic.

Tips

  • Tell students it is fine to bring a photo or drawing if an object is not available.
  • Give every child the same amount of time, around one minute for younger classes.
  • Avoid themes that could embarrass families, such as expensive items.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is it for young children?

Mainly primary school, though older students can use it for short talks.

Can I add themes?

Yes. Add your own, up to 15.

What if a student forgets to bring something?

Let them talk about the theme instead, or draw something during the session.
