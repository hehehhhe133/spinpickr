Brain Break Wheel

Quick movement and calm-down breaks, chosen by a spin.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Short breaks help students refocus, and a spinning wheel turns the break into a small event they look forward to. The wheel starts with eight quick activities that need no equipment.

Ideas and tips

Use it between lessons or when energy drops. Replace the ideas with ones suited to your room and age group, for example quiet options for younger classes or timed challenges for older ones.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again for another result, or change the list first.

FAQ

How long should a brain break be?

Usually one to three minutes is enough to reset attention.

Can students spin it themselves?

Yes, it works well as a student helper job.
