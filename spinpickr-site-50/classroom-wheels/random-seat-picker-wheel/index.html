Random Seating Picker Wheel

Assign seats fairly.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

A new seating plan can freshen up a class and help students meet different classmates, but the way seats are given out matters. A visible random draw makes it clear that no one is being singled out, so there are fewer arguments about who sits where.

The wheel starts with general positions such as front left, back right and near the window. You can replace them with seat numbers, table names or whatever fits your room.

Ways to use it

  • Spin once for each student at the start of term to set a seating plan.
  • Replace positions with the numbers 1 to 15 if you have a numbered layout.
  • Use it for events and exams where seats should not be chosen in advance.
  • Rotate seats monthly with a fresh spin for each student.

Tips

  • Write each result on your own seating chart as you go, since the wheel does not save results.
  • Remove each seat after it is taken so no seat is chosen twice.
  • Set a few seats aside for students with special requirements, such as those who need to sit near the front.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is it a full seating chart tool?

No. It is a simple picker. Record the results on your own chart.

Can I add seat numbers?

Yes. Type numbers or labels one per line, up to 15.

How often should seats change?

Many teachers rotate seats every few weeks, but it depends on your class and your teaching style.
