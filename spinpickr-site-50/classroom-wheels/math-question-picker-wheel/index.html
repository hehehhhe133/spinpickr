Math Question Picker Wheel

Choose a question number or topic at random.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Random selection makes practice feel like a game. Use it to choose topics, question numbers or which student solves the next problem.

Tip

Replace the list with question numbers 1 to 15 for homework review.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Can it generate problems?

No, it only picks from your list.

Is it good for revision?

Yes, it keeps topics varied.
