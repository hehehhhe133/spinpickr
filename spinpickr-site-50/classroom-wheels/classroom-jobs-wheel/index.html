Classroom Jobs Wheel

Spin to give out classroom jobs fairly.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Classroom jobs teach responsibility and help a room run smoothly, but giving them out can lead to complaints: 'You always choose the same people.' A visible random draw ends that argument, because everyone can see that the choice was fair.

The starter list holds common jobs in primary classrooms. Replace them with the roles in your own room, and rotate them weekly so every student has a chance to take part.

Ways to use it

  • Spin on Monday to assign the week's jobs.
  • Spin for the job first and then choose the student, or the other way round with a name wheel.
  • Add special roles for events, such as photographer or timekeeper.
  • Use it in secondary classes for roles such as note-taker, timekeeper and spokesperson.

Making the most of it

A job system works best when it feels like a team effort, not a chore list. Put a chart on the wall that shows who has each job this week, and rotate at the same time every week so students know what to expect. For young children, add small pictures beside each job so they can identify it easily, and give students with different strengths different roles over the term. Jobs can also link to learning: the weather reporter practises speaking, the librarian practises organising, and the timekeeper practises reading the clock. Over time, students begin to take pride in their roles, and the classroom becomes more cooperative.

Tips

  • Explain each job clearly and show students how to do it before the first spin.
  • Remove each job after it is assigned so every role has a student.
  • Praise students for completing their job so the system feels valued.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

How often should jobs rotate?

Many teachers rotate weekly, though daily or monthly rotation also works.

Can I add more than 15 jobs?

One wheel holds 15 options. Use a second wheel if you have more roles.

Can I use it for group work?

Yes. Use it to assign group roles such as leader, reporter and timekeeper.
