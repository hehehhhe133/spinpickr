Valentine's Date Idea Wheel

Plan Valentine's Day with a spin.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Valentine's Day plans do not need to be expensive. The wheel balances romantic, cosy and budget-friendly ideas.

Add your own favourites or things you have never tried together.

Ways to use it

  • Plan the day with a partner.
  • Use it for Galentine's with friends.
  • Add budget-friendly ideas.
  • Mix home and out-of-home ideas.

Making the most of it

The best Valentine's plans are often the ones that feel personal rather than expensive. Use the wheel to narrow things down, then add a small detail that only the two of you would appreciate: a favourite song playing during dinner, a note tucked into a coat pocket, or a dessert from the place where you first met. If you are planning for a long-distance partner, add video-call ideas such as watching the same film together or cooking the same recipe on a call, and let the spin choose which one you try. Spinning together also makes the planning part of the fun.

Tips

  • Agree on a budget before you spin.
  • Book in advance if the idea involves a restaurant.
  • Add a personal touch.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is it only for couples?

No. Friends can use it for Galentine's too.

Can I add my own ideas?

Yes.

Can I plan a whole day?

Yes. Spin for morning, afternoon and evening.
