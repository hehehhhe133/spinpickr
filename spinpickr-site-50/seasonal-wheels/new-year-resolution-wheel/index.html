New Year Resolution Wheel

Spin for a resolution to focus on.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Too many resolutions are hard to keep. Pick one theme with a spin and make it specific and small, and you are far more likely to still be doing it by February.

The default list covers common goals such as exercise, reading, saving money and sleeping earlier. After the spin, write down one concrete action for the first week so the idea turns into a habit.

Ways to use it

  • Pick a single theme for the year.
  • Spin monthly for a small focus.
  • Use it with family for shared goals.
  • Add your own specific goals.

Making the most of it

A resolution only works if it is clear what to do on a Tuesday afternoon in February. After the spin, turn the theme into an action that is small enough to do every day: 'read more' becomes 'read ten pages after dinner', and 'save money' becomes 'move 500 into savings every payday'. Tell one person about your plan, and put a reminder in your calendar for the first week of each month to check how it is going. If the resolution stops fitting your life, spin again. Changing the goal is better than quietly giving up on every goal.

Tips

  • Write one concrete action for the first week.
  • Choose a measurable target.
  • Review progress after a month.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Are resolutions worth it?

They work best when they are small and specific.

Can I add my own?

Yes, up to 15.

When should I spin?

Any time. Many people spin on New Year's Eve or in the first week of January.
