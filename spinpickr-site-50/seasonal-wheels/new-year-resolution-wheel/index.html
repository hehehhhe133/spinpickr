New Year Resolution Wheel

Spin for a resolution to focus on.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Too many resolutions are hard to keep. Pick one theme with a spin and make it specific and small.

Tip

After the spin, write down one concrete action for the first week.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Are resolutions worth it?

They work best when they are small and specific.

Can I add my own?

Yes, up to 15.
