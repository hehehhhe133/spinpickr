Halloween Costume Idea Wheel

Spin for a costume idea.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Choosing a costume can drag on until the last minute. The wheel gives you a quick answer with room to add your own twist.

Tip

Take the result and customise it, for example a witch with a funny hat.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Can kids use it?

Yes, with parents choosing the options.

Can I add group costumes?

Yes, write them as options.
