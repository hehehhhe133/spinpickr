Halloween Costume Idea Wheel

Spin for a costume idea.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Choosing a costume can drag on until the last minute. The wheel gives you a quick answer, with room to add your own twist so it still feels original.

The starter list holds classic costumes that are easy to put together from clothes you already own. Add group ideas, trending characters or funny puns for a bigger mix.

Ways to use it

  • Pick a costume for yourself or the children.
  • Add group costumes for friends and families.
  • Choose a theme for a party.
  • Run a costume contest among friends with everyone spinning for their own outfit.

Making the most of it

The best costumes are usually simple ideas executed with a little personality. If the wheel lands on 'Ghost', think about how you could make it yours: a ghost chef, a ghost librarian, or a ghost with sunglasses. Check what you already own before you buy anything, since a few pieces from your wardrobe, some face paint and one good prop go a long way. For children, choose comfortable, flame-resistant fabrics and avoid masks that limit their view, and add reflective tape if they will be out after dark. Groups can spin together to make a themed set, such as a pirate crew or a monster family.

Tips

  • Customise the result, for example a witch with a funny hat.
  • Check costume safety for children, such as visibility and comfortable shoes.
  • Start early so you have time to gather props.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can kids use it?

Yes, with parents choosing the options.

Can I add group costumes?

Yes. Write them as options.

Is it only for Halloween?

No. It works for any fancy dress party.
