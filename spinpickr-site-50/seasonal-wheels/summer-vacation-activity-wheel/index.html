Summer Vacation Activity Wheel

Fill your summer break with ideas.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Long holidays can slip away without a plan. Spin once a week for the next activity and enjoy variety all summer.

The starter list mixes outdoor and indoor ideas. Replace them with places near you, such as local parks, markets and pools.

Ways to use it

  • Plan one activity each week for the school holidays.
  • Let the children take turns spinning.
  • Add local day trips and attractions.
  • Mix free and paid ideas.

Making the most of it

A summer wheel works best when it matches the rhythm of your holiday. Many families spin on Sunday evening and treat the result as the highlight of the week, then fill the other days with smaller, simpler plans. If you have children of different ages, make one wheel for the whole family and a second for activities the older ones can do alone, such as cycling or a library visit. Keep a few low-cost options in the mix for the weeks when money is tight, and swap out any activity that has been done so the wheel stays fresh until the new term begins.

Tips

  • Mix free options with paid ones so every week fits your budget.
  • Check the weather before outdoor activities.
  • Keep a rainy-day backup.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.
