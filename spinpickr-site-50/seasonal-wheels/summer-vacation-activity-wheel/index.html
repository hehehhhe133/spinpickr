Summer Vacation Activity Wheel

Fill your summer break with ideas.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Long holidays can slip away without a plan. Spin once a week for the next activity and enjoy variety all summer.

Tip

Mix free options with paid ones so every week fits your budget.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Is this for kids or adults?

Both, just edit the list.

Can I keep spinning?

Yes, as often as you like.
