Holi Party Game Wheel

Spin for the next Holi game.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

A Holi gathering is full of energy, but the activities can run out quickly, and then everyone drifts towards the snack table. The Holi Party Game Wheel keeps the celebration moving: spin, announce the next game, and let people join in.

The starter list mixes active games, like tug of war and sack race, with quieter ones, like trivia and colour guessing, so there is something for children, adults and elders. You can edit the list to match your space, your group size and the equipment you have.

Ways to use it

  • Run a game round every 20 to 30 minutes and spin to choose the next one.
  • Split guests into teams with the group picker and use the wheel to decide the game they play.
  • Add songs or dance styles to the wheel for a dance-off round.
  • Let the youngest guest do the spin to build excitement.

Making the most of it

Good party hosts plan for energy levels. Start with lively games while everyone is fresh, move to team activities in the middle and finish with something relaxed, such as trivia, music or a group photo. Keep a small prize box with sweets, small toys or vouchers so winners feel rewarded, and give older guests a role such as judge or scorekeeper so they feel included. If your celebration is in an apartment society or community hall, check the rules about water and colours beforehand, and keep a bucket or cloth ready for spills so the space stays safe and clean.

Tips

  • Use skin-safe, herbal colours and keep them away from eyes and mouths. Offer water and take breaks in the shade.
  • Respect personal choice: only play colours with people who want to, and let others join the games without colours.
  • Protect phones and valuables in waterproof pouches, and put snacks and drinks in a dry corner.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is this suitable for children?

Yes. Remove any game that is not safe for their age, and add simple ones such as musical chairs.

Can I use it indoors?

Yes. Choose games like trivia, dance-off and guessing games, and remove the water-based ones.

Does it work for other festivals?

Yes. Replace the games with ones that suit Diwali, Navratri, Christmas or a birthday party.
