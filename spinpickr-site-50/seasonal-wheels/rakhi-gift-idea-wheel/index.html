Rakhi Gift Idea Wheel

Spin for a Raksha Bandhan gift idea.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Raksha Bandhan is a celebration of the bond between siblings and cousins, and choosing a gift often becomes a last-minute scramble. The Rakhi Gift Idea Wheel gives you a quick shortlist, so you can spend your time on the part that matters: making the day feel special.

The default list works for gifts in both directions, from sisters to brothers and from brothers to sisters. Replace the options with ideas that match the person, whether it is a younger sibling, an older cousin or a sibling who lives far away.

Ways to use it

  • Spin for a gift for each brother, sister or cousin on your list.
  • Add budget labels so every result fits what you can spend.
  • Use it as a starting point and then add something personal, such as a note or a photo.
  • If you live far from your sibling, add gifts that can be sent by post or delivered online.

Making the most of it

The best Raksha Bandhan gifts reflect the relationship rather than the price. If your brother is a cricket fan, a small gift linked to his favourite team means more than an expensive item that could be anyone's. If your sister loves reading, a book with a note about why you chose it will mean something. Siblings can also spin together and agree on a shared gift for their parents, since the festival is a family occasion. For siblings living abroad, plan the shipping early, and a video call during the ceremony is a lovely addition to any gift.

Tips

  • Order early if you are sending a gift to another city; delivery is slower around the festival.
  • Pair any gift with a handwritten message. It costs nothing and is often the part people keep.
  • Think about what the person enjoys, such as books, music, fitness or food, and put those on the wheel.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can brothers use it too?

Yes. The ideas work for any sibling or cousin, whoever is giving the gift.

Can I add my own gift ideas?

Yes. Add up to 15 options, one per line.

What if the result does not suit the person?

Spin again or use the result as a category, for example 'Book' becomes their favourite author's latest title.
