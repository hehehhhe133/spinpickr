Seasonal Wheels

Holiday and seasonal decisions, from gifts to costumes.

Holidays bring a rush of small decisions: what to buy, what to wear, what to cook, who brings which dish. The seasonal wheels here help you get through those choices quickly, with starter lists that you can adapt to your own traditions.

Spin early in the season to give yourself time to shop, plan and prepare. Each wheel can be edited, so you can add local customs, family favourites or ideas that match your budget.

Every wheel here is free and fully editable. Pick one, replace the options with your own and spin. Nothing is stored, so your lists stay private.

Tips for using these wheels

  • Start planning early so you have time to act on the results.
  • Add a price or a note to each option to keep the choices realistic.
  • Save ideas that come up and reuse them next year.
