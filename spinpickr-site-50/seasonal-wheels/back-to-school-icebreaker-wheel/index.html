Back to School Icebreaker Wheel

Start the school year with a fun question.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

New classes work better when students feel at ease. Icebreaker questions on a wheel make the first week friendly and low-pressure.

Tip

Let a student spin for the next speaker to make it feel like a game.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Which age group suits it?

Primary through high school, with age-appropriate edits.

Can I add my own questions?

Yes.
