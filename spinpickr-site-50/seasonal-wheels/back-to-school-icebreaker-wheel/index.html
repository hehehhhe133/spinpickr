Back to School Icebreaker Wheel

Start the school year with a fun question.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

New classes work better when students feel at ease with each other. Icebreaker questions on a wheel make the first week friendly and low-pressure, because the question comes from the spin and not from the teacher putting someone on the spot.

The starter questions are simple and fun, from favourite food to hidden talents. Let a student spin for the next speaker, and the activity feels like a game rather than an introduction round.

Ways to use it

  • Use it on the first day of school to learn names and interests.
  • Run it as a quick starter at the beginning of lessons during the first week.
  • Let students spin for each other to build confidence and spread attention around the room.
  • Add subject-related questions, such as 'Favourite book?' or 'Best science fact you know?'.

Tips

  • Let students pass if a question feels too personal.
  • Answer a question yourself to set a warm tone and show what a good answer looks like.
  • Keep the questions short and age-appropriate, and refresh them for each new class.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Which age group suits it?

Primary through high school, with age-appropriate edits to the questions.

Can I add my own questions?

Yes. Type them one per line, up to 15 on the wheel.

How long should it take?

Five to ten minutes per session is enough, and you can spread it over several days.
