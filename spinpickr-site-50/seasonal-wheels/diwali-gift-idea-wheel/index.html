Diwali Gift Idea Wheel

Spin for a Diwali gift idea.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Diwali means a long list of people to remember: family, friends, neighbours, colleagues, the building staff. Choosing something suitable for each of them can take more energy than the festival itself. The Diwali Gift Idea Wheel gives you a quick starting point, so you can move from 'what should I give?' to 'where do I buy it?' in a single spin.

The default options are the kinds of gifts that fit most households: sweets, diyas, dry fruits, lights, candles and rangoli supplies. Replace them with items that match your budget and the person you are buying for, or keep separate wheels for family, friends and office.

Ways to use it

  • Spin for each colleague or neighbour to keep gifting quick and fair.
  • Make one wheel for elders and one for children, with options that suit each group.
  • Add price tags, such as 'Dry fruits box (500)', so every result stays within your budget.
  • Use it with the Secret Santa wheel for an office gift exchange with a Diwali theme.

Making the most of it

Gifts feel best when a small personal detail is attached. If the wheel lands on 'Diya set', add a handwritten wish; if it lands on 'Mithai box', choose the sweets that the person actually likes rather than a standard assortment. For colleagues and acquaintances, keep the value similar so nobody feels left out, and a modest budget is perfectly fine. Families sometimes spin together to decide the group gift for grandparents, which turns the planning into a shared activity. Whatever you choose, deliver it with a greeting, since the thought is what people remember.

Tips

  • Check expiry dates and ingredients on sweets and dry fruits, and ask about allergies before you buy.
  • Choose reusable or eco-friendly packaging where you can; a simple cloth bag or a decorated box looks festive and creates less waste.
  • Order or shop early. Popular items sell out in the days before the festival.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it for office gifts?

Yes. Add options that suit your workplace budget and spin once for each colleague, or use it to choose one gift for the whole team.

Can I add my own gift ideas?

Yes. Type your own ideas one per line, up to 15 on the wheel.

Is it only for Diwali?

No. The same list works for other festivals and celebrations if you change a few options.
