Yes or No Wheel

A simple wheel for quick decisions.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

When a choice is truly 50-50, a coin or wheel saves time. Notice how you feel when it lands, as that reaction often shows what you really wanted.

It is best for everyday decisions that are not worth agonising over, such as what to order or whether to go out.

Ways to use it

  • Settle small disagreements quickly.
  • Add 'Maybe' or 'Ask again' for a Magic 8-ball style.
  • Use it as a game with friends.
  • Break a tie when two options are equal.

Making the most of it

The real value of a yes-or-no wheel often shows in your reaction. If the wheel says 'Yes' and you feel relieved, you probably wanted to go ahead; if it says 'No' and you feel disappointed, that tells you something too. Use it for harmless decisions, like whether to try a new cafe or take the long way home, and keep it away from choices about health, money or relationships. For a more playful version, add slices such as 'Ask again later', 'Definitely' and 'Only if you share dessert', and turn the spin into a party game with friends.

Tips

  • Pay attention to your reaction after the spin.
  • Repeat 'Yes' on more slices if you want it to win more often.
  • Keep important decisions away from the wheel.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is it a good way to make big decisions?

It suits small choices. For major decisions, think carefully and ask someone you trust.

Is it truly 50-50?

Yes, with two options each has equal chance.

Can I add more answers?

Yes. Add Maybe, Not now or anything else, up to 15.
