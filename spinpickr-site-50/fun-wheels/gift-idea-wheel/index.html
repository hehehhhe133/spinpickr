Gift Idea by Budget Wheel

Spin when you are stuck on what to give.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Gifting stress usually comes from too many options. The wheel narrows them down fast, and you can tailor the list to the person you are shopping for.

The starter list holds eight simple, widely appreciated presents. Replace them with items that match the recipient's interests, and use different lists for different budgets.

Ways to use it

  • Pick a quick gift for a colleague or teacher.
  • Create one list per budget so every result fits what you can spend.
  • Add hobby-specific gifts for a friend or family member.
  • Use it alongside the Secret Santa wheel for office exchanges.

Making the most of it

A thoughtful gift does not need to be expensive, only well matched. Before you spin, think about three things: what the person enjoys, what they use every day and what they would never buy for themselves. Put gifts from those categories on the wheel, and use the result as a starting point for a more personal choice: if you land on 'Book', pick the genre they love; if you land on 'Plant', choose one that suits their space. Add a short handwritten note, and wrap it neatly. For colleagues and acquaintances, keep to a small, standard budget so everyone feels treated equally.

Tips

  • Think about the person first and fill the wheel with things they would genuinely like.
  • Add a handwritten note to make any result feel personal.
  • Include an 'experience' option, such as a cinema voucher, for people who have everything.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Does it suggest products?

No. The options come from you or the defaults.

Can I add my own ideas?

Yes. Edit the list freely.

Is it good for last-minute gifts?

Yes. One spin gives you a direction in seconds.
