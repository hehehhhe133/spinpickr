Workout of the Day Wheel

Let a wheel choose today's workout.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Choosing a workout can be harder than doing it. This wheel removes the choice and gets you moving.

The list holds bodyweight exercises that need no equipment. Replace it with moves you enjoy and can do safely.

Ways to use it

  • Spin for the main exercise of the day.
  • Spin twice: exercise first, then a second list for count or duration.
  • Use it for a home circuit with one spin per round.
  • Share it with workout partners.

Making the most of it

Consistency matters more than the perfect plan, and the wheel helps by making the first decision automatic. Try building a short routine: spin three times, do each exercise for 40 seconds with 20 seconds of rest, and repeat the round twice. Beginners should choose gentler moves, and anyone returning from injury or with a health condition should check with a doctor or trainer first. You can also add a few 'recovery' slices, such as a walk or a stretching session, so your hardest days are balanced with easier ones. Over a week, the random mix naturally covers different muscle groups without any planning.

Tips

  • Warm up before you start and stop if something hurts.
  • Keep a rest slice, such as Stretching, in the mix.
  • Choose moves that match your fitness level.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a fitness plan?

No. It is just a fun way to pick exercises. Check with a professional for personal advice.

Can I add my own moves?

Yes, up to 15.

Can I set the number of reps?

Yes. Make a second wheel with numbers and spin again.
