Truth or Dare Wheel

Spin for a truth, a dare, or a surprise.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

This wheel handles the first decision in truth or dare so nobody can wriggle out of it. Spin to see whether the next person gets a truth, a dare or a lucky skip.

Replace the options with actual questions and dares for a faster game: put ten of your own on the wheel and spin. Keep everything friendly and agree on a few ground rules before you start.

Ways to use it

  • Use it at parties and sleepovers to keep the game moving.
  • Add your own truths and dares, one per line.
  • Run it as a team-building game with workplace-friendly prompts.
  • Include a 'Skip' slice for a lighter feel.

Making the most of it

Good truth or dare games rely on trust and a bit of structure. Before you begin, agree what is off limits, and make sure the dares are safe and easy to do in the room you are in. Replace the default slices with questions and dares written by the group, then mix light and silly ones with a few thoughtful ones so the game does not stay on the surface. For a younger crowd, stick to funny actions like singing a verse or impersonating an animal. Skip slices are useful: knowing there is a way out makes it easier for people to take part.

Tips

  • Agree on ground rules, such as no unsafe or embarrassing dares.
  • Keep dares age-appropriate for the group.
  • Let players pass if they are uncomfortable.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for kids?

The default options are mild. Choose your own questions to fit the group.

Can I add my own dares?

Yes, one per line, up to 15.

What does 'Double dare' mean?

It is a bonus slice; the player does two dares, or you can change the rule.
