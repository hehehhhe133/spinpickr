Truth or Dare Wheel

Spin for a truth, a dare, or a surprise.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

This wheel handles the first decision in truth or dare so nobody can wriggle out of it. Spin to see whether the next person gets a truth, a dare, or a lucky skip.

Ideas and tips

Replace the options with actual questions and dares for a faster game: put ten of your own on the wheel and spin. Keep everything friendly and agree on a few ground rules before you start.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again for another result, or change the list first.

FAQ

Is it suitable for kids?

The default options are mild. Choose your own questions to fit the group.

Can I add my own dares?

Yes, one per line, up to 15.
