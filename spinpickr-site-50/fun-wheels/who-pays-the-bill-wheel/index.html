Who Pays the Bill Wheel

A light-hearted way to settle the check.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Settling the bill with a spin is a friendly tradition among groups. Replace the names and add an 'Everyone splits' slice if you want mercy.

It works best as a bit of fun among friends who are happy with the idea, not as a way to avoid paying your share.

Ways to use it

  • Decide who pays for coffee or lunch.
  • Add a 'Split equally' slice so luck cannot be too costly.
  • Use it for a treat round among colleagues.
  • Spin for who pays for dessert only.

Making the most of it

A good bill game depends on everyone agreeing before the food arrives. Tell the table the rules at the start: who is in the draw, whether the winner pays everything or only their own share, and whether there is a split slice for mercy. Groups that play regularly often keep it to coffees and desserts so the stakes stay small and the fun stays high. It also works well as a thank-you tradition, where the person who pays this time picks the place next time. If anyone at the table would rather not take part, simply let them opt out and share the bill normally.

Tips

  • Agree before ordering so nobody backs out.
  • Keep stakes friendly; a coffee round is better than a big dinner.
  • Use the split slice more than once to keep it kind.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is this gambling?

No. It is a casual game. Please keep stakes friendly.

How many people can join?

Up to 15.

Can I limit the amount?

Yes. Agree on a cap before the spin.
