Who Pays the Bill Wheel

A light-hearted way to settle the check.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Settling the bill with a spin is a friendly tradition among groups. Replace the names and add an Everyone splits slice if you want mercy.

Tip

Agree before ordering so nobody backs out of the result.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Is this gambling?

No, it is just a casual game. Please keep stakes friendly.

How many people can join?

Up to 15.
