Fun & Everyday Wheels

Games, plans and small daily decisions settled by a spin.

Some decisions are not worth the time they take: which film to watch, who does the dishes, what to do on Saturday. The wheels on this page settle everyday choices in one spin and make the answer feel fair to everyone in the room.

They suit couples, families, friends and flatmates alike. Use them to plan the weekend, choose a gift, break a tie or add a little fun to a long car journey, and edit any list so it matches your life.

Every wheel here is free and fully editable. Pick one, replace the options with your own and spin. Nothing is stored, so your lists stay private.

Tips for using these wheels

  • Agree before the spin that the result is final; it saves arguments later.
  • Keep serious choices, such as money or health decisions, away from the wheel and use it for everyday ones.
  • Customise each list so every option is something you would be happy to do.
