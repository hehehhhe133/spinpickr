Netflix Night Picker Wheel

Spin to choose what to watch tonight.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Scrolling for half an hour before choosing is a common modern problem. Spin first, then pick from that category.

The wheel works with any service, since it picks categories rather than titles. If you prefer, add specific shows or films and let the wheel choose one.

Ways to use it

  • Pick the type of show for a night in.
  • Add your watchlist titles to choose between them.
  • Use it with a partner or flatmates to end the debate.
  • Include 'Rewatch a favourite' for zero-risk evenings.

Making the most of it

Choice overload is real: when a streaming service shows thousands of titles, the easiest thing is to do nothing or to scroll endlessly. A category wheel shrinks the problem in one step. Once it lands on 'Documentary' or 'Comedy series', you only have to scan a small slice of the catalogue. For couples and groups, let each person add one category they would enjoy and remove any that someone really dislikes, so the spin is always acceptable. Add 'Rewatch a favourite' for comfort nights, and add 'Short film' for evenings when you have less time.

Tips

  • Give yourselves five minutes of browsing after the spin, then commit.
  • Add a 'Surprise me' slice for whoever wants to take the lead.
  • Keep the list short so each category has a real chance.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Does this connect to Netflix?

No. It is a standalone wheel and works for any streaming service.

Can I add titles?

Yes. Add names instead of categories.

Can I use it to choose the next episode or series?

Yes. Add series names and spin for the next one.
