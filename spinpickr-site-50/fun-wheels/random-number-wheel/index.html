Random Number Wheel

Spin to pick a number from 1 to 10.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

A wheel makes random numbers visible and fun. Everyone watches the spin, which is why it suits games, classroom activities and casual raffles.

The default wheel covers 1 to 10. For other ranges, replace the list, for example with 1 to 15, or use groups of numbers and spin again.

Ways to use it

  • Choose a student number in class.
  • Run a casual raffle among friends.
  • Decide the order of play in a board game.
  • Pick a question number for a quiz.

Making the most of it

A visible spinning wheel has one big advantage over a hidden random generator: everyone can watch the result being decided, which makes it feel fair. That matters in classrooms, family games and friendly contests where people care about trust. If you need numbers above 15, split the range across several spins, or make a wheel of ranges and then a second wheel of the numbers inside the chosen range. For repeated draws without repeats, delete each number as it comes up. Keep in mind that it is meant for casual use; for official raffles or anything involving prizes of value, follow local rules.

Tips

  • For bigger ranges, use groups such as 1-10 and 11-20, then spin a second time within the group.
  • For two-digit numbers, use one spin for the tens and another for the units.
  • Remove a number after it is picked to avoid repeats.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it for raffles?

Yes, for casual raffles where it is allowed.

How many numbers fit?

Up to 15.

Is each number equally likely?

Yes. Every slice has the same chance.
