Weekend Activity Wheel

Not sure what to do this weekend? Spin.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Weekends vanish when nobody decides. The wheel gives you a plan in one spin, and the mix of lazy and active options keeps things balanced.

Edit the list to match your city, your budget and who you are with. Couples, families and friend groups can all use it.

Ways to use it

  • Plan the weekend on Friday evening with one spin per day.
  • Add local places such as parks and cafes you have been meaning to visit.
  • Combine a restful and an active activity.
  • Use it with friends to pick a group outing.

Making the most of it

A good weekend mixes something you look forward to, something that rests you and something that gets a job done. The wheel makes it easy to build that mix: spin once for Saturday, once for Sunday, and keep a third slot free for whatever you feel like. If you live in a busy city, fill the list with places within a short travel time so a spin never turns into an hour on the road. For families, take turns letting each person add one idea per month, and cross off activities you have done so the list keeps moving forward.

Tips

  • Add one productive item and one purely restful one so every weekend feels complete.
  • Write costs next to options, such as 'Cafe (budget 300)'.
  • Agree that the result is final when a group is using it.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can a group use it?

Yes. Agree beforehand that the result is final.

Can I add costs?

Yes. Write options like Cafe (budget 300).

How many options can I add?

Up to 15.
