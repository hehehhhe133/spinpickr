Secret Santa Name Picker

Draw names for your gift exchange, fairly.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Secret Santa works best when the draw is clearly fair. Type everyone's name into the wheel and spin once per person to see who they are buying for.

Ideas and tips

Do the spins privately, or let each person spin on their own phone. If someone lands on their own name, simply spin again. Agree on a budget before you start so gifts feel balanced.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again for another result, or change the list first.

FAQ

What if someone gets themselves?

Spin again. The draw only counts when it is someone else.

Is it private?

Nothing is saved or sent anywhere, so the result stays on your screen.
