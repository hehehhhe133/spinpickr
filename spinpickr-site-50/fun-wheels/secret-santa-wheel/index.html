Secret Santa Name Picker

Draw names for your gift exchange, fairly.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Secret Santa works best when the draw is clearly fair. Type everyone's name into the wheel and spin once per person to see who they are buying for.

Do the spins privately, or let each person spin on their own phone. Agree on a budget before you start so gifts feel balanced.

Ways to use it

  • Run the draw for an office or a friend group.
  • Let each person spin on their own device.
  • Set a budget and a gift theme, such as handmade or under 500.
  • Use it for family gift exchanges at holidays.

Making the most of it

A smooth Secret Santa starts before the draw. Agree the budget, the gift deadline and the date of the exchange, and collect everyone's names in one place so nobody is forgotten. During the draw, make sure each person takes a private spin and writes down their result before anyone else sees it. If someone lands on their own name, spin again. For remote teams, run the draw on a call where each person turns their screen away from the camera, then reveal the gifts on a video call. A short wish list from each person, kept anonymous, makes gift choosing much easier.

Tips

  • If someone lands on their own name, spin again.
  • Keep results private by not showing the screen to others.
  • Write down assignments straight away, since the wheel does not store them.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

What if someone gets themselves?

Spin again. The draw only counts when it is someone else.

Is it private?

Nothing is saved or sent anywhere, so the result stays on your screen.

How many people can join?

Up to 15 names per wheel.
