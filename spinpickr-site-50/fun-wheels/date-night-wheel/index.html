Date Night Idea Wheel

Out of ideas? Let the wheel plan your evening.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

After a while, deciding what to do together takes longer than doing it. This wheel keeps date nights fresh by choosing from ideas you both approve of.

Ideas and tips

Fill it with options in a mix of budgets and effort, and agree beforehand that the result is final. Couples often keep a weekly "spin night" so planning never becomes a chore.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again for another result, or change the list first.

FAQ

Can we add our own ideas?

Yes, replace the list with your own favourites.

Does it work for friends too?

Yes, it works for any group.
