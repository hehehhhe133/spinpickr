Date Night Idea Wheel

Out of ideas? Let the wheel plan your evening.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

After a while, deciding what to do together takes longer than doing it. This wheel keeps date nights fresh by choosing from ideas you both approve of.

Fill it with options across a mix of budgets and effort, and agree beforehand that the result is final. Couples often keep a weekly 'spin night' so planning never becomes a chore.

Ways to use it

  • Let each partner add three ideas, then spin to choose.
  • Plan a monthly date night from a shared list.
  • Use it for double dates or nights out with friends.
  • Keep a mix of free and paid ideas so there is always something doable.

Making the most of it

The best date nights are not always the most elaborate ones. A wheel helps because it takes pressure off both people: neither has to be the planner, and the result is a shared surprise. Fill it with a balance of at-home and out-of-home ideas, and keep a few last-minute options for weeks when you are both tired. Agree who handles the practical bits for each activity, such as booking or buying ingredients, so the evening runs easily. After a few months, look back at which ideas you enjoyed most and replace the ones that never got picked.

Tips

  • Agree on a budget range before spinning.
  • Add a 'wildcard' slice where the winner gets to choose.
  • Update the list every few months so it stays interesting.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can we add our own ideas?

Yes. Replace the list with your own favourites.

Does it work for friends too?

Yes. It works for any group.

What if we dislike the result?

Agree on a single re-spin rule beforehand, so the wheel stays fair.
