Rainy Day Activity Wheel

Stuck indoors? Let the wheel find something to do.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Rainy days can feel long without a plan. The wheel offers cosy, low-effort ideas for both children and adults, so you spend less time deciding and more time enjoying the day.

The starter list includes indoor classics like baking, puzzles and hot chai. Replace them with whatever you have at home.

Ways to use it

  • Pick a family activity for a wet weekend.
  • Let children spin for a craft or a game.
  • Choose a solo activity for a quiet afternoon.
  • Add chores like 'Declutter' for a productive rainy day.

Making the most of it

A rainy day is easiest when the activities are prepared before the clouds arrive. Keep a box with craft supplies, a couple of board games and a few baking staples, and your wheel will never land on something you cannot do. For families, let each child add one idea to the wheel and take turns spinning, so everyone feels part of the plan. Adults can use the same list for a slower day: a long bath, an unhurried meal, a film or an hour of reading. Add the time each activity takes so you can fit two or three into one afternoon.

Tips

  • Keep supplies for two or three items at home so you are always ready.
  • Set a time for each activity to keep the day varied.
  • Add one screen-free option to balance the list.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can kids use it?

Yes, with an adult nearby.

Can I add indoor games?

Yes. Edit the list.

Does it work for school holidays?

Yes. Spin each morning for the day's main activity.
