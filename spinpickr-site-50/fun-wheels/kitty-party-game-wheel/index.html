Kitty Party Game Wheel

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

A kitty party works best when there is a rhythm to the afternoon: time to chat, time to eat, and a few games that get everyone laughing. Hosts often struggle to choose games that suit everyone, and some guests are always hesitant to volunteer. The Kitty Party Game Wheel decides for you.

The starter list holds games that need little equipment and suit mixed ages, such as tambola, Name Place Animal Thing and a Bollywood song guessing round. Edit it with your group's favourites, and add a few new ones to keep the meetings fresh.

Ways to use it

  • Spin before each round and announce the game with a bit of drama.
  • Choose the host or the next game's leader with a second wheel of names.
  • Add themed rounds, such as '90s songs' or 'Guess the dish', for a different feel each month.
  • Use it for birthday parties, baby showers and anniversary celebrations too.

Making the most of it

Over a year of kitty parties, the same games can start to feel repetitive. Keep a shared list of games that have been played, and let each host add one new idea to the wheel. Rotate who runs the games so one person does not carry the whole afternoon, and agree on a small prize budget so every host feels comfortable. For online or hybrid groups, choose games that work on a video call, such as trivia, guess the song and the alphabet game. A simple rule that helps is to start with an easy icebreaker, move on to a team game and finish with a group favourite, so everyone leaves in a good mood.

Tips

  • Keep one or two prizes ready for each game, such as sweets, small plants or accessories.
  • Plan about three or four games, with ten to fifteen minutes each, so the afternoon does not feel rushed.
  • Pick games that include shy guests and avoid anything that could embarrass someone.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it for baby showers or birthdays?

Yes. Replace or add games that suit the occasion.

Do I need any equipment?

Most of the starter games need only paper and pens, music or a phone.

How many games should I plan?

Three or four is usually enough for a two to three hour event.
