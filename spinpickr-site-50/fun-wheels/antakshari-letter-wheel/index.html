Antakshari Letter Wheel

Spin for the starting letter of your next song.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Antakshari is one of the easiest ways to bring a room together: two teams, a few songs and a lot of laughter. The only trouble is that the game stalls when someone cannot think of a song for a difficult letter. The Antakshari Letter Wheel gives you a fresh starting point whenever the game slows down.

Spin to choose the letter, then let a team sing a song that begins with it. The starter list uses letters that are common in Hindi song titles, and you can replace them with any other letters or even themes, such as 'Kishore Kumar songs' or '90s hits'.

Ways to use it

  • Start a new round with a spin so the opening letter is a surprise.
  • Use it when the usual chain breaks and nobody can find a song.
  • Add themes like 'Old classics', 'Romantic songs' or 'Party songs' for extra variety.
  • Play it on car journeys, at get-togethers and in family WhatsApp video calls.

Making the most of it

The best antakshari rounds mix familiarity with a few surprises. Include some themed rounds so that older and younger guests have something to contribute: songs from the 70s for grandparents, recent hits for teenagers, and devotional or regional songs if your group enjoys them. If you play with children, allow nursery rhymes or film songs they know. For a competitive evening, add a bonus round where the wheel chooses a letter and both teams get one turn to sing. When voices are tired, switch to a hum-the-tune round, where one person hums a melody and the rest guess the song.

Tips

  • Agree on a time limit, for example ten seconds, for the team to start singing.
  • Decide beforehand whether a line or a full verse counts, and whether film names are allowed.
  • Keep a scoreboard and a small prize for the winning team.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Does it include all letters?

No. The default list holds eight common letters. Replace it with any letters you like, up to 15 on a wheel.

Can I use it for other languages?

Yes. Use the letters or themes that match your language and songs.

Can I use it as a theme wheel?

Yes. Replace the letters with themes such as '90s', 'Rafi songs' or 'Rain songs'.
