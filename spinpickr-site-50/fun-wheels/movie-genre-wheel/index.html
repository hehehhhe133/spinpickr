Movie Genre Picker Wheel

Decide the genre first, then the film.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Arguing over the film often starts with not agreeing on a genre. Let the wheel pick the genre, then choose from that shorter list.

Tip

Everyone nominates one title in that genre and you spin again to select the winner.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin.
  • Spin again, or change the list first.

FAQ

Can I add language options?

Yes, for example Hindi, English, Korean.

Is it good for groups?

Yes, it keeps things fair.
