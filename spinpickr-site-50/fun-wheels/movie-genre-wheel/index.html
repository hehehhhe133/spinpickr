Movie Genre Picker Wheel

Decide the genre first, then the film.

One option per line. Min 2, max 15 options.

About this wheel

Arguing over the film often starts with not agreeing on a genre. Let the wheel pick the genre, then choose from that shorter list.

It works for couples, families and friend groups, because the decision comes from the wheel and nobody has to give in.

Ways to use it

  • Pick the genre for a movie night, then browse only that category.
  • Ask each person to nominate one title in that genre and spin again to select the winner.
  • Add languages such as Hindi, English or Korean for a bigger mix.
  • Use it for a movie marathon, one spin per film.

Making the most of it

A genre wheel gives a group something that real arguments rarely have: a result that nobody can blame on another person. Spin it before you open any app, and let each person suggest one title in the winning genre. If suggestions tie, use a second wheel with the titles to decide. For families, add genres that include the children, such as animation, adventure and family comedy, and set an end time so the film suits everyone's bedtime. When you want something new, add genres you rarely watch, such as documentary, musical or classic, and give the film at least twenty minutes before deciding.

Tips

  • Add a 'free choice' slice so the lucky person gets to pick any film.
  • Remove genres someone strongly dislikes before spinning.
  • Set a time limit for browsing once the genre is chosen.

How to use it

  • Edit the options in the box under the wheel, one per line.
  • Press the centre button to spin. Use the speaker icon in the corner of the wheel to mute the sound.
  • Spin again, or change the list first. Nothing is stored, so refreshing the page resets everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add language options?

Yes, for example Hindi, English or Korean.

Is it good for groups?

Yes. It keeps things fair.

Does it recommend specific films?

No. It chooses the genre; you choose the title.
