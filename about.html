About SpinPickr

SpinPickr was built to solve a simple problem: making a choice shouldn't require an account, a signup, or a cluttered screen full of pop-ups.

Whether you're deciding what to eat, who goes first in a game, or what to watch next, SpinPickr gives you a fast, clean, and completely free decision wheel that runs instantly in your browser.

No data is stored, no options are sent to a server, and no nonsense. Just the wheel.
